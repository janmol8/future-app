Privacy Policy

Future Chart is an educational paper trading simulator. It uses virtual money only and is not investment advice.

Data We Collect

We may store account email, hashed password, lesson progress, subscription status, watchlists, alerts, paper trades, and simulator journal entries.

Market and AI Providers

Market data may be requested from third-party providers such as yfinance, NSE endpoints, or crypto data APIs. If AI commentary is enabled by an administrator, selected chart or simulator context may be sent to the configured AI provider for educational discussion. Do not enter sensitive personal data into AI prompts or journals.

Payments

Paid subscriptions are processed by Razorpay or the app-store billing provider configured for distribution. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider, not stored by this app.

Your Choices

You can use the free simulator without enabling optional AI commentary. Contact the app operator to request account deletion or data export.
